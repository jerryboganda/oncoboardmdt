Why this exists

Cancer care is decided in one meeting. The tools in it were built for something else.

Researchers sat in on 96 lung cancer MDT meetings across eight teams in one UK cancer network. They found inefficiencies in every one of them.

24%

of case discussions ended with the decision deferred, most often because results were still pending.

21%

of meetings observed were fully quorate. Palliative care was the specialty most often missing.

60%

of cases discussed had fitness or performance status information available, ranging from 30% to 75% by team.

Source: Zasada et al., Cancer Medicine, 2023. 8 lung MDTs, 96 meetings, 12 weeks.Read the studyNHS England streamlining guidance, 2020

The same board, two Tuesdays.

NHS England's 2020 guidance asks teams to give full discussion to complex cases and manage the rest against agreed standards of care. That only works if each case arrives prepared and leaves recorded.

    FAQ

    Asked in
    every demo.

    Does OncoBoard replace our PACS?
    No. It reads DICOM in the browser for board review: windowing, slice navigation and calibrated measurements. Primary diagnostic reporting stays on your PACS workstation.
    We already meet on Teams or Zoom. Do we stop?
    Keep your call for audio and video. OncoBoard handles what a screen share can't: one synced slice for everyone, the dossier, the votes and the signed record.
    Do patient names ever enter the system?
    Cases are identified by MRN and initials, and the case record has no field for a full name. Uploaded DICOM files are read in your browser and not sent to the server, but their headers can still carry a name, so upload de-identified exports.
    Can the AI make or change a decision?
    No. Every AI output is labelled and ends with a disclaimer. The assistants draft summaries, slice reads and minutes, and answer questions. Voting, ratifying and editing the record are clinician actions only.
    What is on record after a case is discussed?
    Each specialty's vote with its rationale, the ratified recommendation, the minutes, any RECIST measurements and an audit trail. The consensus report exports per case for your EHR.
    How does the demo work?
    Thirty minutes, online. We set up your boards and roles in a sandbox and run a de-identified sample case from intake to a signed consensus report, with your team driving.
