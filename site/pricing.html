Pricing questions

Before you
ask for a quote.

What counts as a board?
One recurring tumour board, such as your Thoracic Board, with its own case list, schedule and imaging presets. The Multi-Organ board comes with Cancer Centre and Network.
Is there a limit on clinicians?
No. Everyone who sits on a licensed board gets their own account, including the coordinator, visiting specialists and trainees who present cases.
Is AI included?
Yes, all four assistants are in every plan. They run on the Gemini API key your organisation configures, so model usage sits under your own agreement with Google. Leave the key unset and the rest of OncoBoard works as normal.
Can we start with one board and add more?
Yes. Start on Board, then move to Cancer Centre when the next board is ready. Cases, votes and the audit log stay in the same database.
Where does OncoBoard run?
On Node.js and MySQL, on infrastructure your organisation approves. The security page sets out what we provide and what stays with you.
What does the demo cover?
Thirty minutes, online. We set up your boards and roles in a sandbox and run a de-identified sample case from intake to a signed consensus report. Bring the details that shape a quote: which boards, which sites and who sits on them.
