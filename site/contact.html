Security & governance

FAQ

Before
you book.

Who should come to the demo?
The chair, the coordinator and one clinician from each specialty that votes. Add someone from IT or information governance if they will review the setup.
Do we need to share any patient data?
No. The session uses a de-identified sample case. Please do not send identifiable patient information through this form or during the session.
Which boards can we see?
Any of Thoracic, Neuro, Breast, GI, Heme and Multi-Organ. Each opens with its own imaging presets, so choose the boards you run in the form.
What does it cost?
It depends on the boards and sites you run. The pricing page sets out what each plan includes.
Can our IT team review the architecture?
Yes. OncoBoard runs on Node.js and MySQL, and the security page covers data handling, sign-in, sessions and the audit log. Bring their questions to the session.
