01 · Data

De-identified by design.

A case is identified by its MRN and the patient's initials. The case record has no field for a full name, date of birth or address, so there is nowhere to type one.

  • Stored: MRN, initials, age, sex, board, urgency and status.
  • Stored: TNM, AJCC stage group, ECOG, pathology, IHC, NGS variants, labs, treatment history and trial matches.
  • Stored: saved measurements, votes, minutes, case chat and the audit log.
  • Not sent to the server: uploaded DICOM files. They are parsed in the clinician's browser.

AI governance

The AI can draft.
It cannot decide.

OncoBoard's four assistants use the Google Gemini API. These are the limits they work inside.

Called on request

The server calls the model only when a clinician presses an assistant button. Nothing runs in the background, and without an API key configured nothing is sent at all.

What leaves the server

Case synthesis and case questions send the de-identified case context. A slice read sends a rendered image of the current slice, not the DICOM file. Minutes send the votes and recent case discussion.

Labelled and disclaimed

Every output is headed "AI Decision Support" and ends with a disclaimer, wherever it appears.

Cannot act on the record

Assistants cannot vote, sign off or edit a case. A clinician reads the draft and does each of those.

On the audit log

Synthesis, slice reads and drafted minutes are written to the audit log with the clinician who asked.

Your key, your choice

AI runs on an API key your organisation configures on the server. Leave it unset and the rest of OncoBoard works as normal.

Architecture

One server.
One database.

OncoBoard is a React 19 web app, a Node.js and Express server with a WebSocket hub, and a MySQL database. The recommended deployment serves it over HTTPS and WSS.

Questions

Asked by
governance teams.

Where is patient data stored?
In the MySQL database of the deployment you approve. Uploaded DICOM series are parsed in the clinician's browser and are not sent to the server; only measurements you choose to save are stored.
Does OncoBoard store patient names?
The case record has no field for a full name, date of birth or address. Cases carry an MRN and initials. DICOM headers and free-text fields are the two places a name could still appear, which is why we ask for de-identified exports and a local rule on free text.
Is any data sent to an AI provider?
Only when a clinician presses an assistant button. The server then sends the de-identified case context, or a rendered image of the current slice, to the Google Gemini API using the key your organisation configures. With no key configured, no AI request is made.
Can clinicians type identifiers into free text?
Yes. The clinical summary and case chat store what is typed. Your local policy should say identifiers stay out of free text, in the same way it does for email and meeting notes today.
What does the audit log record, and what doesn't it?
It records clinical changes and AI drafts: cases created and updated, measurements saved and deleted, sessions scheduled, votes cast, consensus signed, AI synthesis, slice reads and minutes, and new clinician accounts. It does not record who viewed a case.
Which certifications does OncoBoard hold?
This page describes only the controls that exist in the product today. We cover certification and assurance status in the demo, with your information governance lead in the room.
