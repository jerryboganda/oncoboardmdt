Privacy notice

This notice explains how OncoBoard MDT ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this marketing website. It does not cover the OncoBoard MDT application that clinical teams sign in to. Data in the application is handled under the agreement with the organisation that runs it.

What we collect

  • Demo requests. When the live demo form is enabled, we receive what you enter: first and last name, work email, organisation, role, the boards you run, approximate weekly case numbers, when you want to start, an optional note, and your consent to be contacted. On this design preview, the form sends nothing.
  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark, the site stores your choice in your browser's local storage under the key ob-b-theme. It stays on your device. We never receive it, and you can clear it by clearing site data in your browser.
  • No cookies. This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Please do not send patient information through this website. The demo uses a de-identified sample case.

Clinical disclaimer

OncoBoard MDT is decision-support software for multidisciplinary tumour boards. It supports clinical judgement. It does not replace it.

  • Decisions stay with clinicians. Treatment recommendations are made by the board and treatment decisions by the treating clinicians, with their patients.
  • AI output can be wrong. Every AI output in OncoBoard is labelled as AI Decision Support and ends with a disclaimer. It must be checked against the source record before anyone acts on it. AI cannot vote, ratify a consensus or edit the record.
  • Image review is for the board. DICOM viewing, windowing and measurements in OncoBoard support discussion at the board. They are not a substitute for diagnostic reporting on your PACS.
  • Calculations need a check. Stage groups, RECIST sums and response categories are calculated from the data entered. Wrong inputs give wrong results, so review them at the board.

Nothing on this website is medical advice for any individual patient.
