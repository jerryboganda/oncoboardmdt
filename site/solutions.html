01 · Thoracic Oncology

Resect, irradiate, or target first?

Stage III lung cancer is where a thoracic board earns its time. The answer depends on how many mediastinal stations are involved, whether there is a targetable driver, and what PD-L1 shows. All three sit beside a CT that opens in a lung window.

What the board argues about

  • Is the N2 disease single-station and resectable, or multistation?
  • Is there an actionable driver before immunotherapy is considered?
  • Is the patient fit for concurrent chemoradiation?
  • Is the target lesion measurable and reproducible for RECIST?

02 · Neuro Oncology

One integrated diagnosis.

Since the 2021 WHO classification of CNS tumours (CNS5), the molecular result is part of the diagnosis. An IDH-mutant astrocytoma with CDKN2A/B homozygous deletion is CNS WHO grade 4, whatever the histology shows. The board needs the molecular report on screen before it can name the tumour.

Study default
Brain MRI · CT brain window 40 / 80 when CT is reviewed
Framework
WHO CNS5 (2021) integrated diagnosis
Markers
IDH1/2 · 1p/19q · CDKN2A/B · MGMT promoter · H3 K27 · ATRX · TERT promoter

What the board argues about

  • Is this an IDH-wildtype glioblastoma on molecular criteria, even if the histology looks lower grade?
  • Is the 1p/19q codeletion confirmed for an oligodendroglioma diagnosis?
  • Is the MGMT promoter methylated, and how does that change the temozolomide discussion?
  • What does the post-operative MRI show about the extent of resection?

03 · Breast Oncology

HER2-zero or HER2-low?

HER2 used to be positive or negative. HER2-low (IHC 1+, or 2+ with negative ISH) now opens a treatment option in metastatic disease, so the exact score matters. Receptors, Ki-67 and germline results sit beside the breast MRI before the board weighs neoadjuvant treatment against surgery first.

Study default
Breast MRI
Staging
AJCC TNM · grade
Receptors
ER · PR · HER2 IHC 0 / 1+ / 2+ / 3+ with ISH
Markers
Ki-67 · BRCA1/2 · PIK3CA

What the board argues about

  • Neoadjuvant systemic treatment or surgery first?
  • Is HER2 zero, low or positive, and was ISH done for a 2+ result?
  • Does a germline BRCA1/2 result change the surgical or systemic plan?
  • Is breast-conserving surgery realistic after response on MRI?

04 · GI Oncology

Resectable, borderline, or not yet?

With colorectal liver metastases the surgeon's question comes first: how many lesions, where, and how much liver would remain. The study opens in a liver window, measurements are calibrated in millimetres, and mismatch repair and RAS/BRAF status are on the same screen when the question is asked.

Study default
CT liver · Liver 60 / 160
Staging
AJCC TNM · CEA trend from labs
Markers
dMMR / MSI-H · KRAS · NRAS · BRAF V600E · HER2

What the board argues about

  • Are the liver metastases resectable now, or only after conversion chemotherapy?
  • Is the tumour dMMR/MSI-H, pointing towards immunotherapy?
  • Is RAS wild-type, so anti-EGFR treatment is an option?
  • Is BRAF V600E present, and what does that do to the sequence?

05 · Heme Oncology

Response, risk, and the next line.

Lymphoma boards read PET-CT against the Lugano classification and its Deauville five-point scale. Leukaemia boards weigh ELN 2022 genetic risk before deciding on transplant. Both need imaging, cytogenetics and molecular results in one dossier.

Study default
PET-CT
Frameworks
Lugano · Deauville 1–5 · ELN 2022
Markers
NPM1 · FLT3-ITD · TP53 · MYC / BCL2 rearrangement

What the board argues about

  • Is the interim PET Deauville 1–3, a complete metabolic response, or 4–5?
  • Does a MYC and BCL2 rearrangement change the regimen?
  • Where does the AML sit on ELN 2022 risk, and is transplant in first remission on the table?
  • Is the patient fit for intensive treatment?

06 · Multi-Organ Board

When the primary isn't obvious.

Cancer of unknown primary, oligometastatic disease and second primaries need every specialty at once. The Multi-Organ board brings every window preset and every marker set to one agenda, so pathology can work through the IHC panel while radiology scrolls the staging scan.

Study default
Any of the six clinical presets
Staging
Per suspected primary
Markers
Full IHC panel · NGS tiers I-A to III

What the board argues about

  • Does the IHC panel point to a likely primary?
  • Is the disease oligometastatic, and is treating every site realistic?
  • Is this a second primary or a metastasis?
  • Is there an actionable variant whatever the site of origin?
