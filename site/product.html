01 · Case Registry

Every case, by board, urgency and status.

The coordinator registers a case once: MRN, initials, board and urgency. TNM entries resolve to an AJCC stage group as they are typed. From then on the case moves through four states that the whole board can see.

Statuses
Intake → Scheduled → In Review → Consensus Reached
Urgency

SLA target

Identity

  • Filter by board, status and urgency, or search by MRN.
  • Press ⌘K or Ctrl+K to jump straight to any case.

02 · Patient Dossier

The whole case on one page.

Pathology, molecular results, labs, treatment history and trial matches sit in structured fields on the case. The board reads the pathologist's findings the way they were written, next to the variants that change the options.

Pathology
Specimen, biopsy site and date, histology, grade, margins, IHC, pathologist note
NGS
Gene, variant, VAF, tier I-A / I-B / II-C / III, actionable therapy
Labs
Value, unit, reference range, flag Normal / Elevated / Critical / Low
Trials
NCT id, phase, biomarker match, Eligible / Screen Required / Ineligible
  • Treatment history by date, modality, regimen and outcome.
  • Case chat keeps questions for the board on the case itself.

04 · Live Board Room

One slice. Every screen. One vote each.

The presenter drives the slice and the window, and every connected clinician follows in real time. Each specialty votes with a rationale. Once three specialties have voted, quorum is met and the chair can sign off.

Presence

Output

  • Case timer you can start and pause.

  • AI Draft Minutes, edited by the chair before sign-off.

05 · AI Decision Support

Four assistants. No vote between them.

The assistants read the case and write the drafts a board never has time for. A clinician starts each one. Every output is labelled, ends with a disclaimer, and stays a draft until a clinician acts on it.

Assistants
Case synthesis · Slice read · Consensus minutes · Case Q&A
Provider
Google Gemini API, called by the server
When
Only when a clinician triggers an assistant
Limits
Cannot vote, sign off or edit the record
  • Every output labelled AI Decision Support.
  • Minutes are edited and signed by the chair.

Built for the room

Used at 07:30,
on whatever screen is there.

Boards meet in daylight clinics, dark reporting rooms and on laptops in corridors. The details below are why it holds up in all three.

Clinical Light and Dark Radiology

A daylight theme for clinic rooms and a low-glare theme for reporting rooms. The image viewer stays dark in both.

Command palette

⌘K or Ctrl+K finds any case, board or view. Arrow keys to move, Enter to open.

Keyboard and screen readers

Every control has a label and a visible focus state, and tabs, dialogs and toasts are announced.

Reduced motion respected

If your system asks for less motion, the interface settles instantly. Motion never sits on the image.

Works on a phone

The registry, dossier and votes fit a phone screen, for the clinician who joins from the ward.

Every change confirmed

Case registered, RECIST saved, vote cast, consensus signed: each one shows a confirmation, so nobody wonders if it worked.

How each part works, in one table. Read the security overview

AreaHow it works
Application
Front endReact 19 single-page application. Clinical Light and Dark Radiology themes.
ServerNode.js with Express. Built with npm run build, started with npm start.
Real-timeWebSocket rooms per session carry presence and the presenter's slice, window and measurements.
DatabaseMySQL in production. SQLite for local development.
Clinical data
ImagingDICOM series parsed in the browser: slice navigation, window and level, calibrated measurements, tag viewer.
Identity of patientsMRN and initials only. No field for a full name.
AIGoogle Gemini API, called by the server only when a clinician starts an assistant. Output is labelled and disclaimed.
Access and record
AuthenticationPasswords hashed with scrypt and a per-user 16-byte salt, compared in constant time.
Sessions256-bit random bearer tokens. Expire after 12 hours, revoked at sign-out.
AuditClinical changes written to an audit log with who and when, visible in Audit & Database.
ConfigurationEnvironment variables for the database connection and the AI key. Secrets stay in your hosting environment.
