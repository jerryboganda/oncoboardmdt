Glossary

The words
on the board list.

Plain definitions for the terms that come up in every meeting, for new members and for the people who support them.

MDT
Multidisciplinary team. The meeting, often called a tumour board, where specialists agree a treatment recommendation for each patient's case.
Quorum
The minimum set of specialties needed for a valid recommendation. In OncoBoard, quorum is met when three specialties have voted.
RECIST 1.1
Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumours, version 1.1. Rules for measuring target lesions and calling complete response, partial response, stable disease or progressive disease.
AJCC TNM
The American Joint Committee on Cancer staging system. Tumour (T), nodes (N) and metastasis (M) combine into a stage group such as Stage IIIA.
ECOG
Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group performance status, from 0 (fully active) to 5 (dead). It records how fit a patient is for treatment.
VAF
Variant allele frequency. The proportion of sequencing reads that carry a given variant, reported as a percentage.
NGS tiers
The AMP/ASCO/CAP scheme for somatic variants. Tier I has strong clinical significance, Tier II potential significance, Tier III unknown significance and Tier IV is benign or likely benign.
PD-L1 TPS
Tumour proportion score. The percentage of viable tumour cells showing partial or complete membrane staining for PD-L1.
Deauville scale
A five-point scale for FDG PET-CT in lymphoma. It compares uptake in residual disease with the mediastinal blood pool and the liver.
Standards of Care
In NHS England's 2020 streamlining guidance, agreed statements of the treatment a patient should expect for a defined presentation, used to decide which cases need full discussion.
